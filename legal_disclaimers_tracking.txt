# Legal Disclaimers Tracking

This file tracks the legal warnings and disclaimers added to the Africa Market Hub application.

## 1. Transporter Liability Clause
**Text:** "Transporters operate independently. Africa Market Hub is not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged goods."
**Locations:**
- Transport Booking Screen (`src/components/TransportBusinessProfile.tsx`): Added as a warning box and a required checkbox before confirming a booking in the Request Transporter modal.
- Transporter Signup Page (`src/components/BecomeTransporterModal.tsx`): Added as a required checkbox in the Guarantor step and a disclaimer in the Payout step.

## 2. Property Ownership Clause
**Text:** "Africa Market Hub does not verify ownership of listed properties. Users must conduct due diligence before making payment."
**Locations:**
- Property Details Page (`src/components/PropertyAgencyProfile.tsx`): Added as a warning box right above the "Message" button.
- Property Upload/Signup Page (`src/components/BecomePropertyOwnerModal.tsx`): Added as a warning box in the Payout step so property owners accept responsibility.

## 3. Business Staff Responsibility Clause
**Text:** "Business owners are responsible for actions of their staff accounts."
**Locations:**
- Business Add Staff Page (`src/components/dashboard/PropertyDashboard.tsx`): Added as an info box when creating a staff login.
- Business Registration Page (`src/components/BusinessOnboarding.tsx`): Added as a required checkbox in the Review & Submit step.

## 4. Transporter Guarantor Clause
**Text:** "Transporters may be required to provide guarantor details for accountability."
**Locations:**
- Transporter KYC Page (`src/components/BecomeTransporterModal.tsx`): Added as an info box in the Guarantor section.
